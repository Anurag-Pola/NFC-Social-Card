The tap moment

One tap — or one scan. Same landing card.

The NFC chip carries a smart URL that opens a hosted landing card — vCard save, WhatsApp shortcut, our properties, and a direct booking channel. No app needed. The QR on the back encodes the identical URL, so anyone on an older handset — or holding the card across a table — still gets there.

Brand mark

Supplied artwork, used as-is.

Both cards carry the real gold-gradient emblem. victory-emblem.svg is the mark alone for the card faces; victory-lockup.svg adds the “Victory Hotels & Resorts” wordmark for signage and the landing page. Both are cropped views of the identical supplied artwork — no redraw, no re-encode.
